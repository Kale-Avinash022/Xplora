# Video Background

Place your travel background video here as `travel-bg.mp4`

The video should be:
- Format: MP4
- Recommended resolution: 1920x1080 or higher
- Recommended duration: 10-30 seconds (it will loop)
- File size: Keep it under 10MB for best performance
- Content: Travel-related footage (landscapes, cities, transportation, etc.)

Once you add the video file, it will automatically play as the background in the hero section.
